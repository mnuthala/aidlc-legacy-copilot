# Story clarity and quality
Clarity dimensions: actor_purpose, scope, behaviour, acceptance_criteria, dependencies.
Quality dimensions: value, completeness, testability, feasibility, consistency, traceability, size.
For EACH dimension supply score, reason and evidence. Anchors: 0 missing; 1 major ambiguity; 2 partially defined/material gaps; 3 clear/minor gaps; 4 explicit, consistent and evidence-backed.
Index = sum(scores)/(4*number of dimensions)*100. Default readiness requires both indices >=85 and no blocking questions. Scores are review aids, not probability or statistical confidence. No aggregate overrides a material blocker. The independent reviewer must read the BRS/code, not rely on author self-ratings. Every requirement, story and acceptance criterion must have task/test coverage. The controller checks structure and links, not semantic truth; human review remains necessary.
